Woah: Recovery & Training

Getting sick? A radar for flu season

Your watch often notices a cold before you do. Woah turns that into one question you can answer at breakfast: am I getting sick?

The illness radar scores 1 to 10 how much last night looks like the pattern that often comes a day before people feel ill. It reads up to five signals against your own normal: sleeping wrist temperature, resting heart rate, heart-rate variability, breathing rate and how broken the night was. Every signal is listed, so you see which sit where they usually do and which have drifted. A low score means no signs; several systems off at once and Woah says to take it easy, and eases the coach's planned session for that day.

Looking back, a timeline over weeks and months shows the periods you were run down at a glance.

It is not a diagnosis, and the page says so. The score, the signals and the timeline are free and computed on the iPhone. It works with Apple Watch, Oura and Garmin; without wrist temperature it reads the signals it has and says so.
